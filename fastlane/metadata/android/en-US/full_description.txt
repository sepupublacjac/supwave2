Supwave2 is a music player client built specifically for Navidrome, implementing
Material 3 Expressive (dynamic color, expressive shapes and motion) throughout.

Features:

* Browse and search your music library, with playlists and liked songs
* Real audio playback with a persistent media notification (play/pause/next/stop)
  and a playback session that survives closing the app
* Shuffle, repeat, and queue management, including "play next" from any song's
  context menu
* Offline downloads, so you can listen without a network connection
* Album art caching
* Playlist management: create, delete, and add/remove songs
* Dynamic color and Material 3 Expressive styling throughout

Supwave2 requires your own Navidrome server - it does not include or connect to
any music of its own. See https://www.navidrome.org/ to set one up.
