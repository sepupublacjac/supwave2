New: song lyrics. Open them from the player; synced lyrics highlight the
current line, follow playback and jump the song when tapped. Lyrics come from
your server, or optionally NetEase and lrclib.net (off by default, see
Settings > Lyrics), with translations and an adjustable offset.
Fixes: correct app version in the profile sheet, a song queued twice no
longer breaks the queue, and offline downloads no longer load whole files
into memory.
